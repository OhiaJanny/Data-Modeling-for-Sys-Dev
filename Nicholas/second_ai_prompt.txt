Design an efficient solution for a student-by-quiz score matrix. Find every unique K-quiz combination for which at least P students score at least R on every selected quiz. Compare exhaustive enumeration with a stepwise temporary-structure method. M, N, P, R, and K must be general. Explain correctness, pruning, suitable data structures, and asymptotic time and space complexity. Also recommend a fair experimental procedure for comparing the implementations. Do not use an external database.
